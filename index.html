NOTE: 3+ seats are being onboarded by Brightspeed. If a customer has 3+ seats, please DO NOT ONBOARD as they are being onboarded by Brightspeed TDE team.
Email: Brightspeed-Onboarding@brightspeed.com
A Brightspeed Technical Design Engineer (TDE) will assist with setup, configuration, and admin training.
If callers need immediate technical assistance, Brightspeed’s 24x7 support team is available at 833 363 2313.

Unique Processes

Brightspeed Rules:

  1. Always check SCP for notes if the account is for Brightspeed TDE onboarding (3+ DLs). If so, DO NOT ONBOARD.
  2. If the caller's account total seats is 3+ but was previously 0-2, onboard the customer.
  3. Put the start and end of warranty period in USER notes in SCP after completion and CCNR (Audited).
